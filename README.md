## 🛠 About

I am a **Lead Systems Administrator** with a strong **Site Reliability Engineering mindset**, focused on operating **reliable, secure, and observable cloud platforms** in Microsoft Azure.

My work centers on:
- Treating infrastructure as a production system
- Designing for failure and fast recovery
- Reducing operational risk and human toil through automation
- Clear communication during incidents and change

While my formal title has been systems-focused, much of my day-to-day work aligns closely with **SRE practices**: incident response, monitoring and alerting, CI/CD-driven change, and continuous reliability improvement.

- **Automation instead of manual runbooks**
